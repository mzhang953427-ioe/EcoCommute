Methodology

Learn about how your commute data is calculated.

Distance and Time Conversions

When you enter travel time, we estimate your distance using typical speeds for each mode of transport in Dublin, based on current conditions. Similarly, when you enter distance, we estimate your travel time. Cars and buses have a rush hour option if you are travelling during rush hour or on congested roads, and we take into account of this slower speed and potentially higher emissions, while DART and Luas maintain typical service speeds as indicated on their websites.

Speeds and times are rigourously calibrated to current Dublin data. We use a variety of methods, including official website reports, real-life experiments and Google Maps data to accurately estimate times and distances for each mode of transport.

Air Quality Exposure Score

This is a cumulative index from 0 to 100 based on:

  • Mode of Transport: Car, bus, DART and Luas generally have lower exposure to pollutants and traffic exhaust than walking and cycling.
  • Traffic Environment: Proximity to busy roads or waiting at bus stops increases exposure to pollutants and traffic exhaust.
  • Rush Hour: Rush hour commuting, especially walking and cycling, tend to increase exposure to pollutants and traffic exhaust, due to the volume of vehicles.
  • Duration: Longer commute times increase exposure to pollutants and traffic exhaust.
  • Perception: Personal, subjective ratings that reflect each individual's commute environment.

Data Privacy

Your commute details are processed entirely in your browser using local logic and algorithms. No personal identifiable information (PII) or exact location coordinates are transmitted to external servers, ensuring complete privacy. Any data submitted to your school's dashboard is anonymous and not personally identified to any individual, and all such data is stored securely in our databases.

Alternative Routing Logic

Alternative route options are not reflective of your specific route, and although it automatically omits illogical alternatives, some modes of transport may not be available. It gives a comparison of travel time and carbon footprint based on available data, recalculating travel times using dynamic transit speeds based on whether your specific travel window overlaps with Dublin's congestion hours. Please ensure you research alternative route options carefully.

Our Purpose

Climate change is one of the most important crisis facing our world today, and its devastating impacts have already been felt by communities around the world. Transport produces nearly 8 billion tonnes of CO₂ every year, when in fact, this number could be significantly lower if we truly understand the scale of the impact of daily commuting.

EcoCommute was developed to help students across Dublin to calculate their annual carbon footprint, and provides accurate estimates based on current data, with alternative route options available to help students to understand how their carbon footprint could be reduced. Students also have the option to see their school dashboard, enabling them to make informed decisions about their environmental impact.
